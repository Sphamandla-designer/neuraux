Project overview.

KIY Trucking needed a professional website to present its transport and logistics services to prospective business customers.

AX-Channels designed and implemented the company's website using WordPress, bringing its service offering, company information and contact pathways together in one digital experience.

The project focused on creating a professional online presence that communicates the business's services clearly and gives prospective clients a way to make enquiries.

The approach.

Four parts, from how the services are presented to how a customer gets in touch.

  • Service presentation

    Structured the website around the company's transport and logistics offering, helping visitors explore its services and understand the scope of the business.

  • Website design

    Developed the website's visual presentation and page layouts to give the company a consistent, professional digital presence.

  • WordPress implementation

    Translated the design into a working WordPress website, implementing the pages and website functionality required for the project.

  • Enquiry pathways

    Included contact and enquiry elements to give prospective customers a direct way to get in touch with the business.

The deliverable.

AX-Channels delivered a live corporate website for KIY Trucking, combining website design and WordPress implementation.

The completed website provides a central place for prospective clients to explore the company's services, learn about the business and find its contact information.

Client feedback.

The project received client feedback following delivery. This provided qualitative feedback on the work, rather than a measured assessment of business performance.

Project outcome.

The project resulted in a completed, live website for KIY Trucking.

It demonstrates AX-Channels' ability to take a client website project from design through implementation, delivering a functional digital presence for a business operating in the transport and logistics sector.
