Your AI may be working. Your experience may not be.

Most teams test whether an AI system can produce an answer. Fewer test what happens when the answer is ambiguous, incomplete, uncertain, sensitive or simply wrong. That's where the experience breaks.

  • It doesn't understand.

    The intent is ambiguous, and it guesses.

    “I need to change my details.” Which details?

  • It answers too quickly.

    It responds before it has collected enough context.

    Gives the generic answer when the customer's product changes it.

  • It sounds more certain than it is.

    Users can't tell a confident answer from an uncertain one.

    An estimate and a fact read exactly the same.

  • It doesn't know when to stop.

    Sensitive or high-risk situations carry on without escalation.

    A fraud report gets a cheerful FAQ link.

  • It fails to recover.

    One misunderstanding becomes a dead end.

    “Sorry, I didn't understand,” three times in a row.

  • The handoff loses context.

    The customer has to repeat everything to a person.

    The agent starts with “How can I help you today?”

What we audit: six dimensions.

Each dimension is scored from 1 to 5, for a total out of 30, with evidence from real conversations behind every score. Select a dimension to see what we look for.

Clarity

Can users understand what the AI can and cannot do, and does it understand what they're asking?

What we look at
The opening message, how scope is explained, and how ambiguous requests are handled.
Example
A user types “I need to change my details.”
Failure state
The assistant guesses which details, and updates the wrong thing.
Recommended improvement
Clarify before acting: offer the likely options in one short question.

Trust

Can users judge when to rely on an answer?

What we look at
AI disclosure, how confidence is expressed, and whether sources are shown.
Example
A user asks what an early withdrawal will cost.
Failure state
A general figure is given as if it applies to this customer's account.
Recommended improvement
Signal uncertainty: say what's general, show the source, and offer a way to confirm.

Recovery

What happens when the AI misunderstands, can't help, or needs a person?

What we look at
Fallback messages, repeated misunderstandings, and whether there is always a route to a person, with context.
Example
The assistant can't add a joint account holder in chat.
Failure state
“Sorry, I can't help with that.” No reason, no next step.
Recommended improvement
Explain, offer, hand over: say why, offer a safe alternative, and pass the conversation to a person.

Efficiency

Do tasks get done in the fewest sensible turns?

What we look at
Up to 15 core tasks, walked through end to end.
Example
A signed-in customer updates their address.
Failure state
The assistant asks for details it already has, then confirms them twice.
Recommended improvement
Use known context: remove redundant questions and confirm once.

Tone

Does it sound like your brand, and suit the moment?

What we look at
Voice consistency, and how tone shifts in sensitive or stressful moments.
Example
A customer reports a card they think was stolen.
Failure state
An upbeat greeting and an emoji, followed by a link to the FAQ.
Recommended improvement
Tone rules per moment: calm, direct, and the urgent action first.

Compliance safety

Does the AI recognise where it should not continue?

What we look at
Advice boundaries, disclaimers, and how personal information and consent are handled.
Example
“Should I sign the settlement agreement?”
Failure state
The answer reads like legal advice.
Recommended improvement
Hold the boundary: say what it can't advise on, offer general information, and escalate to a person.

From conversation data to a prioritised action plan.

  1. Understand

    We review the product, audience, business goals and the existing AI journey.

  2. Analyse

    We examine up to 200 anonymised conversations and walk through up to 15 core tasks, including edge cases and failure states.

  3. Identify

    We find patterns across the six dimensions and back each finding with evidence.

  4. Prioritise

    We rank findings by severity and separate quick wins from structural changes.

  5. Recommend

    You receive the report and fix list, and we walk your team through it in a recorded readout.

Questions about the Audit.

What is an AI Experience Audit?

A two-week, fixed-price review of one customer-facing AI experience on one channel. We score it across six dimensions, find where users get confused, stuck or poorly handled, and give you a severity-ranked report and a prioritised fix list.

How do you audit an AI chatbot?

We review up to 200 anonymised conversations and walk through up to 15 core tasks end to end. We look at trust and transparency, failures and recovery, tone and brand voice, and compliance-sensitive moments. Every finding is backed by evidence from the conversations.

What does an AI UX audit include?

A report with findings ranked by severity, each with evidence and a fix; a scorecard across six dimensions; a prioritised fix list that separates quick wins from structural changes; and a recorded readout with your team.

How long does an AI Experience Audit take?

Two weeks.

How much does an AI UX audit cost in South Africa?

The NeuraUX AI Experience Audit is a fixed R18,500, excluding VAT, paid 50% on signing and 50% on delivery. Scope and price are agreed in writing before we start.

Can you audit WhatsApp AI experiences?

Yes. Our method is platform-agnostic, and each Audit covers one channel, so WhatsApp can be that channel. We need test access to the experience and anonymised transcripts.

Can you audit an AI assistant before launch?

Yes, as long as it can be tested. We walk through the core tasks end to end and review test or pilot conversations. We agree in the proposal exactly what we'll review.

Do you need access to customer conversations?

We need anonymised transcripts, up to 200, and test access to the AI experience. We don't need access to your other systems.

Can you work with anonymised data?

Yes. We only work with anonymised transcripts, with no unredacted personal information. We sign NDAs on request, and project files are deleted 90 days after close.

Do you provide development?

No. Redesign, technical fixes and prompt engineering are out of scope. Your team or agency implements the fixes, and we can recommend build partners.

What happens after the audit?

You have the report, the fix list and the recorded readout. Your team can act on the quick wins straight away. For structural changes, a Conversation Design Blueprint designs the fix, and the Audit fee is credited in full toward a Blueprint signed within 30 days of the readout.

What is the difference between an AI Experience Audit and a Conversation Design Blueprint?

The Audit diagnoses an existing AI experience and tells you what to fix first. The Blueprint designs how an AI experience should behave, in a build-ready form your developers can implement. Most clients start with an Audit; if you're building something new, you can start with a Blueprint.
