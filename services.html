See what a finding looks like

What we review

  • Up to 200 anonymised conversations
  • Up to 15 core tasks, walked through end to end
  • Trust and transparency: AI disclosure, confidence, sources, human handoff
  • Failures and recovery
  • Tone and brand voice
  • Compliance-sensitive moments: advice boundaries, POPIA, disclaimers

What you receive

  • A report with findings ranked by severity, each with evidence and a fix
  • A scorecard across six dimensions: Clarity, Trust, Recovery, Efficiency, Tone and Compliance safety
  • A prioritised fix list, separating quick wins from structural changes
  • A recorded readout

Out of scope

Redesign, user testing with real participants, technical fixes, and prompt engineering.
