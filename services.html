What a finding looks like.

Every finding in an Audit report has the same shape: the problem, the evidence, why it matters, and what to do instead.

Sample audit Illustrative example, not a client result
Finding
C-02
Dimension
Compliance safety
Severity
High
Fix type
Structural change
Context
Fintech, product questions

Problem

The assistant gives a vague answer when the user asks a sensitive question.

Evidence

UserIs this savings product a good place for my retirement money?
AssistantIt's a popular choice with competitive returns. Many customers use it for long-term goals.

Why it matters

The user cannot tell whether the answer is safe to rely on. It reads like a recommendation, without the boundary, context or disclosure a financial advice question needs.

Recommended UX response

  • State the limitation
  • Explain what can be answered
  • Provide a safe alternative
  • Offer human escalation

Redesigned response, draft

AssistantI can't tell you whether it suits your retirement, because that depends on your circumstances and is financial advice. I can explain how the product works and what it costs, or book a call with a qualified adviser.
