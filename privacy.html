Last updated 14 October 2026

  • Only what you send

    Our forms open your own email app. We receive only what you choose to send.

    We use your details to respond to your enquiry. We never sell them or add you to a mailing list.

Who we are

NeuraUX is an AI UX and conversation design consultancy based in Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa. This policy covers this website and enquiries you send us. How we handle client data during an engagement is set out on our Data protection page and in each engagement agreement.

What we collect

When you contact us

The contact, Audit and Blueprint forms don't send anything themselves. They open your own email app with your answers filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send. We then receive what you included, which can be your name, work email, company, role, the service you're interested in and any notes you add. We also receive anything you email us directly at hello@neuraux.co.za.

When you browse this website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising. We don't collect information about your visit ourselves.

To show you the site, your browser connects to the services listed under Who else processes it. Like any website host or font service, they receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

The Home FIX prototype

If you open the Home FIX prototype from our case study, it saves your progress in your own browser so a refresh doesn't lose your place. That information stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call.
  • To prepare a proposal if you ask for one.
  • If we work together, to run the engagement and keep the records it needs.

We process this information because you chose to send it to us, to take the steps you've asked for before any agreement, and to run an engagement we've agreed with you. We don't use it for marketing lists, and we never sell or rent it.

Who else processes it

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare serve the typefaces the site uses.
  • Our email provider stores the emails you send us.

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa. For client work, we use business-tier AI tools only, never used to train models on client data, as described on our Data protection page.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry only for as long as we need it to respond to you and follow up. If we go on to work together, project files are deleted 90 days after the engagement closes, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep personal information secure, limit access to the people who need it, and handle it in a POPIA-aware way. For Audits, we work from anonymised transcripts only, with no unredacted personal information.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you can:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and for a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to how we process it, or withdraw your consent;
  • complain to the Information Regulator.

To make a request, email hello@neuraux.co.za. We'll confirm who you are before we act on it.

Contact and complaints

Questions about this policy or your information go to our Information Officer at hello@neuraux.co.za.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
