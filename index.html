07 · About NeuraUX

UX Design→Enterprise Systems→AI Experience→NeuraUX
SP
SphamandlaFounder, NeuraUX

I spent years designing digital products, and kept seeing the same thing happen: a business would bring in a chatbot or automation tool, and within weeks their own team had quietly stopped using it. The tool wasn't broken. Nobody had designed how it should actually work for real people.

That's why NeuraUX exists — we treat AI as a design problem before it's a technology one. South African businesses in particular are underserved here: most AI tools are built for enterprise budgets and Silicon Valley connectivity, not a WhatsApp-first team working around load-shedding and data cost. We'd rather build for that reality than around it.

Common questions

What happens during the discovery call?

We spend 30 minutes looking at one part of your business — enquiries, bookings or admin — and talk through what's actually happening today. You'll leave with a clear view of what to fix first, whether or not we work together.

What do I receive from the AI Experience Audit?

A written findings report, a prioritised list of quick fixes, and a walkthrough call to explain it all in plain terms — usually within two weeks.

How long do projects usually take?

It depends on scope. AI Conversations projects are the fastest to launch; workflow systems and larger experience design engagements take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline before any work begins.
