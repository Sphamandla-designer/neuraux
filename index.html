Trust is won or lost when AI goes wrong.

Most AI assistants are built to function, not to be understood. In legal and financial services, a confident wrong answer is more than frustrating. It's a risk.

  1. It doesn't understand.

    The request is ambiguous. A designed assistant asks the right clarifying question instead of guessing.

    Ambiguous requestFintech onboarding
    User saysI need to change my details.
    Without designYour details have been updated.
    Designed responseWhich details would you like to change: your contact details, your banking details, or your ID document?
    PatternClarify before acting

  2. It doesn't know.

    The answer isn't certain. A designed assistant says so, shows where its information comes from, and offers a way to confirm.

    Uncertain answerAccount query
    User saysWhat's the fee if I withdraw early?
    Without designThe early withdrawal fee is 2%.
    Designed responseFees depend on your product, so I can't confirm yours here. The standard fees are in the published fee schedule. A consultant can confirm the exact amount for your account.
    PatternState confidence, cite the source, offer a check

  3. It shouldn't answer.

    The question crosses an advice boundary. A designed assistant holds the line, stays helpful, and hands over to a person.

    Sensitive requestLaw firm intake
    User saysShould I sign the settlement agreement?
    Without designYes, that sounds like a fair offer.
    Designed responseI can't advise you on whether to sign. I can explain what a settlement agreement usually covers, or arrange a call with your attorney.
    PatternSafe boundary, then human handoff

The same applies when a user needs a person, or shares something sensitive. NeuraUX designs for those moments.

The method

Five areas of practice, applied to every assistant we audit or design.

  • Audit methodology

    A six-dimension scorecard, evidence from real conversations, and findings ranked by severity.

  • Conversation design

    Persona, voice, conversation architecture and detailed flows, including edge cases.

  • Failure-state design

    What the assistant does when it doesn't understand, doesn't know, or shouldn't answer.

  • Human handoff

    When a person takes over, how the user is told, and what context travels with them.

  • Compliance-sensitive UX

    Advice boundaries, AI disclosure, disclaimers and POPIA consent, designed in from the first flow.

Problem

The assistant gives a vague answer when the user asks a sensitive question.

Evidence

UserIs this savings product a good place for my retirement money?
AssistantIt's a popular choice with competitive returns. Many customers use it for long-term goals.

Why it matters

The user cannot tell whether the answer is safe to rely on. It reads like a recommendation, without the boundary, context or disclosure a financial advice question needs.

Recommended UX response

  • State the limitation
  • Explain what can be answered
  • Provide a safe alternative
  • Offer human escalation

Redesigned response, draft

AssistantI can't tell you whether it suits your retirement, because that depends on your circumstances and is financial advice. I can explain how the product works and what it costs, or book a call with a qualified adviser.
