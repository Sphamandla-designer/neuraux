Why NeuraUX exists.

More AI assistants now handle conversations that matter: a client asking about their matter, a customer asking about their money. Most are built to function. Far fewer are designed for the moments they go wrong.

Trust is won or lost in those moments: when the assistant doesn't understand, doesn't know, or shouldn't answer. NeuraUX exists to design for them.

We don't build AI. We design how it behaves.

We work alongside your developers or build agency. They build the assistant; we design the experience around it.

What we leave to your build team

  • Building or integrating the assistant
  • Model selection
  • Final production prompts
  • Technical fixes

What we do

  • Audit AI assistants that are live or in testing
  • Design conversations, flows and edge cases
  • Design trust patterns: disclosure, uncertainty, sources and advice boundaries
  • Design human handoff and recovery
  • Work white-label for AI agencies, under their brand

What makes NeuraUX different.

We focus only on the experience layer of AI, in regulated, high-stakes conversations.

  • Regulated-industry focus

    Advice boundaries, disclosure and POPIA, designed in from the first flow, not bolted on.

  • Product design first

    AI designed as part of the whole experience, not a chat window bolted on.

  • Fixed scope, fixed price

    Published prices, defined deliverables and short timelines, agreed in writing before we start.

  • Transparent about AI

    We say exactly how we use AI, and every deliverable is reviewed and signed off by the lead designer.
